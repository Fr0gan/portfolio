      Questions

      Before you go

      Do I need hiking experience?

      Not for easy trips. Moderate trips suit people who walk regularly. Strenuous trips assume you're comfortable hiking 8+ miles with real elevation gain.

      What happens if the weather turns bad?

      Guides check the forecast the evening before. If a trip is unsafe, we reschedule or refund you in full.

      What should I bring?

      Comfortable footwear, layers, 2 liters of water and any personal medication. We provide snacks, first aid and any loaner gear you need.

      Can I cancel a booking?

      Cancel up to 48 hours before your trip for a full refund. After that, you can move your booking to another date at no charge.
