Amortization schedule

A Lab experiment, written in plain JavaScript. Uses the standard fixed-rate formula with the APR divided by 12 for the monthly rate. Every month's interest is rounded to the cent and the last payment absorbs any rounding, as lenders do, so the schedule adds up exactly. Extra payments go straight to principal. Estimates only: taxes, insurance, PMI and fees aren't included. Your numbers are remembered in this browser and never sent anywhere. View source · More Lab experiments
