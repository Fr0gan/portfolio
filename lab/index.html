Lab

Small tools and experiments, built from scratch in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. They're smaller than the main projects and newest first.

  1. Nov 13, 2026

    Habit Tracker

    Check off daily habits, keep current and best streaks, fill in days you forgot, and see the last 12 weeks in a contribution-style grid.

  2. Nov 10, 2026

    Date Calculator

    Days, weeks, months and weekdays between two dates, or the date a number of days, weeks, months or business days away.

  3. Nov 8, 2026

    JSON Formatter

    Format, minify and validate JSON, with optional key sorting and the exact line and column of any error. Nothing you paste leaves your browser.

  4. Nov 6, 2026

    Grade Calculator

    Your weighted class grade and letter from your syllabus categories, plus the score you need on the final to reach a target grade.

  5. Nov 3, 2026

    Word Counter

    Word, character, sentence and paragraph counts with reading and speaking time, a word goal for assignments, your most-used words and a warning for very long sentences.

  6. Nov 1, 2026

    Password Generator

    Strong random passwords or memorable passphrases, generated on your device with the browser's secure random number generator, with a strength estimate in bits.

  7. Oct 30, 2026

    Focus Timer

    A Pomodoro-style timer with short and long breaks, custom durations, an optional chime and a daily session count. Uses timestamps so it stays accurate in background tabs.
