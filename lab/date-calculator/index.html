A Lab experiment. Dates are handled in UTC so daylight saving changes never shift a day. Business days skip Saturdays and Sundays, and can also skip the 11 US federal holidays under today's rules. A holiday that falls on a Saturday is observed the Friday before, and one on a Sunday the Monday after. Juneteenth counts from 2021 and Martin Luther King Jr. Day from 1986. State and local holidays aren't included. Adding months keeps the day of the month where possible, so January 31 plus one month is the last day of February. View source · More Lab experiments
